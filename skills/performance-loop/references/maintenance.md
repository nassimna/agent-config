# Validating changes to performance-loop

When an app and permitted test target are named, trial the revised skill on an
operation with a known bottleneck and a comparison without it. Keep the
expected cause out of the evaluator's initial brief. Check whether the loop
attributes the delay, rejects noisy or incomparable results, and preserves
behavior. Never introduce load or defects into shared or customer systems.
Without a target, label checks as structural or simulated; they do not prove
bottleneck discovery, verified gains, or production performance.
