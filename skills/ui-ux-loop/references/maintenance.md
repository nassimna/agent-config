# Validating changes to ui-ux-loop

When a suitable app and permitted test environment are named, trial the revised
skill on a small journey with known defects and an acceptable comparison. Keep
the expected issues out of the evaluator's first-pass brief. Compare found and
missed issues, unsupported findings, and coverage against that record. Never
seed defects into shared or customer systems. Without an app, label validation
as structural or simulated; it does not prove real-app discovery.
